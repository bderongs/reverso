Translate while you watch

Hover over any word in the subtitles. YouTube, Netflix, Prime Video or TED become language lessons.

  1. 1

    Turn subtitles on

    Play a video and enable captions in the language you are learning.

  2. 2

    Hover any word

    Move the cursor over a word. The translation appears instantly, so you can keep watching.

  3. 3

    Click for more

    Click the word to see extra translations, examples, pronunciation, and to save it to your Favorites.

Common issues

Subtitles must be on, in the language you are learning — not auto-translated into your native language.

If nothing appears, click the Reverso icon and enable translation in subtitles for this site.

You can also turn on “Translate the full subtitle when I hover over a word” in the extension settings.
