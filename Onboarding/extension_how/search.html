Search

Look up a word from the extension

When you are not on a video or an article — writing an email, thinking of a word, checking a phrase — type it in the Reverso search bar.

  1. 1

    Pin Reverso to the toolbar

    Click the puzzle icon, then pin Reverso. The icon stays visible so you can open search in one click.

  2. 2

    Open the extension

    Click the Reverso icon. The popup opens on top of the page you are on.

  3. 3

    Type and search

    Enter a word or expression in the search bar and hit Search (or Enter).

Where is the icon?

If you only see a puzzle piece, Reverso is installed but hidden. Pin it once — after that, the search bar is always one click away.
