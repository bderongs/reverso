How to use the extension

Reading

Translate anything you read

Select a word, expression, or paragraph on any website. No copy-paste.

  1. 1

    Select the text

    Highlight a single word, an expression, or a longer passage on the page.

  2. 2

    Click the Reverso icon

    A small Reverso icon appears next to your selection. Click it for an instant, contextual translation.

  3. 3

    Go deeper if you want

    Open definitions, listen to pronunciation, and save the word to Favorites so you can review it later with flashcards.

Or double-click a word

Double-click any word on the page to look it up straight away — no need to select it or click the floating icon. Turn this on or off in the extension settings: Look up a word instantly via double-click.

Or even quicker, activate interactive reading mode

Then you can hover a word to see its translation, the same way as in video subtitles. Turn it on from the Reverso menu on the page when you want a more fluid reading session.
