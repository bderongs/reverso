How to use the extension
Video Reading Search

Reading

Translate anything you read

Select a word, expression, or paragraph on any website. No copy-paste.

  1. 1

    Select the text

    Highlight a single word, an expression, or a longer passage on the page.

  2. 2

    Click the Reverso icon

    A small Reverso icon appears next to your selection. Click it for an instant, contextual translation.

  3. 3

    Go deeper if you want

    Open definitions, listen to pronunciation, and save the word to your Favorites.

Alternative Reading options

Double-click a word

Double-click any word on the page to look it up straight away. Turn this on or off in the extension settings.

Interactive reading mode

Turn it on from the sidebar Icon or in the Extension settings and start hovering words when you want a more fluid reading session.
