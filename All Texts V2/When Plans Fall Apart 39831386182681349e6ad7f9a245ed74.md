## Text

Last Saturday, I had planned a small birthday picnic for my sister in the park. When heavy rain started just as we arrived, everyone looked <em>disappointed</em>, and I felt a sharp <em>disappointment</em> too.

I tried to move the food under a shelter, but the wind kept blowing napkins away, and I became <em>frustrated</em> and <em>annoyed</em>. My sister was <em>upset</em> because two friends canceled at the last minute, and my dad grew <em>irritated</em> while trying to keep the cake dry.

For a moment, I felt <em>discouraged</em> and completely <em>fed up</em> with the whole day. I could tell my sister was <em>bummed out</em> and <em>out of sorts</em>, even though she kept saying it was fine.

Later, we went home, ordered pizza, and played old board games in the living room. I was <em>regretful</em> that I had not checked the weather more carefully, but by evening we were laughing, and I realized a ruined plan can still become a warm memory.
