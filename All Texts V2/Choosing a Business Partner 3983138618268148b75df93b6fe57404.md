Subject: Thoughts on Marco as a Potential Partner

Hi Elena,

After our meeting yesterday, I wanted to share my <em>honest</em> opinion about Marco. He seems <em>trustworthy</em> and <em>decent</em>, and his former clients described him as <em>loyal</em>, <em>faithful</em> to long-term agreements, and <em>devoted</em> to solving problems rather than avoiding them.

What impressed me most was his <em>integrity</em>. He admitted a past mistake without blaming others, which suggests he has a strong <em>conscience</em> and is <em>committed</em> to doing business responsibly.

Still, we should check whether he is <em>steadfast</em> under pressure. A <em>staunch</em> supporter of our values is useful, but only if he can remain calm, practical, and <em>fair</em> when deadlines are tight.

Overall, I think he is a <em>worthy</em> candidate for partnership. If the references confirm what we have seen, I would feel comfortable inviting him to the next round of discussions.

Best,

Nadia
