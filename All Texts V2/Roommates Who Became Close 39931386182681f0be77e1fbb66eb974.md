By spring, they were an <em>affectionate</em>, <em>tight-knit</em> pair, always <em>laughing</em> about laundry, bills, and bad dates. Their <em>friends</em> joked that they were <em>joined at the hip</em>, but Maya knew it was more than habit; it was <em>friendship</em> built through everyday <em>trust</em>.
