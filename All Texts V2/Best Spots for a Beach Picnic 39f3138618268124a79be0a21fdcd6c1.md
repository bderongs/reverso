## Text

For a relaxed picnic, look for a small <em>bay</em> where the water is calm and the <em>sand</em> is clean. These areas are often more <em>peaceful</em> than the main <em>beach</em>, especially in the morning before families arrive with games and music.

A spot near a <em>cliff</em> can offer shade and a dramatic view, but check local signs before settling down. Bring a <em>towel</em>, a <em>beach chair</em>, and a <em>beach umbrella</em> so you can stay comfortable while enjoying the <em>breeze</em>.

If you prefer a tropical setting, choose a place beside a <em>palm tree</em>, where you can listen to the leaves moving above you. Pack a <em>cooler</em> with fruit, sandwiches, and cold drinks, and avoid places that feel too <em>crowded</em> at lunchtime.

The best picnic may be the one that lasts until <em>sunset</em>, when the sky turns orange and the <em>sea</em> becomes quiet. Before you leave, shake the <em>sand</em> from your things and take all your rubbish with you.
