## Text

With final exams approaching, many students are planning how to study without panic. Teachers advise them to <em>hit the books</em> early, rather than <em>burn the midnight oil</em> the night before or <em>pull an all-nighter</em> that leaves them exhausted.

A smart study plan helps learners <em>cover a lot of ground</em> in small steps. Students can <em>brush up on</em> old notes, <em>bone up on</em> difficult chapters, and <em>catch up on</em> homework they missed during busy weeks.

Experts say it is not enough to memorize facts <em>by heart</em> if students do not understand them. Still, key formulas, dates, and definitions are useful to <em>know by heart</em>, especially when time is limited.

During an exam, stress can make even prepared students <em>draw a blank</em>. Short breaks, practice tests, and clear summaries can help them stay calm and <em>make the grade</em>.

Those who study regularly are more likely to <em>pass with flying colors</em>. In the end, success usually comes from steady effort, not last-minute panic.
