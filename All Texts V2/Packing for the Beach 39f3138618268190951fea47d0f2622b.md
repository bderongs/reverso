Subject: What to bring tomorrow

Hi Maya,

I’m really looking forward to our day by the <em>sea</em> tomorrow. I checked the forecast, and it should be hot and sunny, so please bring <em>sunscreen</em> to avoid <em>sunburn</em>, plus a <em>hat</em> and <em>sunglasses</em> for extra protection.

I’ll pack my <em>swimsuit</em>, a large <em>towel</em>, and my blue <em>flip-flops</em> because the <em>sand</em> may be too hot for bare feet. I’m also bringing a <em>waterproof</em> phone pouch, just in case we decide to take photos near the <em>waves</em>.

Could you bring the <em>beach umbrella</em> and the folding <em>beach chair</em>? I’ll take care of the <em>cooler</em> with fruit, sandwiches, and plenty of cold drinks, which should feel <em>refreshing</em> after <em>swimming</em>.

Let’s meet at the bus stop at 9:00 a.m. If you think of anything else we might need, send me a quick message tonight.

See you tomorrow,

Lena
