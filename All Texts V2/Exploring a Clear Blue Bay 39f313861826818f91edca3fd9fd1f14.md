## Text

Travelers looking for a quiet coastal escape often choose a small <em>bay</em> where the water is <em>crystal clear</em> and the <em>sand</em> remains <em>pristine</em>. From the <em>shore</em>, the <em>ocean</em> shifts from pale blue to deep <em>turquoise</em>, creating a calm scene for swimmers and photographers.

A short boat ride takes visitors to a nearby <em>reef</em>, where bright <em>coral</em> grows in natural patterns beneath the surface. With a simple <em>snorkel</em>, you can watch silver fish move through the rocks, while a <em>starfish</em> rests slowly on the seabed.

Guides remind tourists not to touch the <em>coral</em> or collect <em>seaweed</em>, because both are important to the local ecosystem. On warmer days, a <em>jellyfish</em> may appear near the rocks, so swimmers are advised to stay alert and follow safety flags.

For a wider view, visitors can walk along the path above the <em>cliff</em> and look down at the sheltered water below. Adventurous guests may also <em>dive</em> with trained instructors, discovering a <em>peaceful</em> underwater world just minutes from the <em>beach</em>.
