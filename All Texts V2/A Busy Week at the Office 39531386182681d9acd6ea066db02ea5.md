## Text

On Monday morning, I ran into Maya by the coffee machine and asked how her <em>weekend</em> had been. She smiled, but admitted her <em>workload</em> had doubled because two teammates were on vacation, and she was already feeling <em>swamped</em>.

By Wednesday, everyone seemed <em>slammed</em> with client requests. Tom joked that he was <em>buried</em> under spreadsheets, while Nina said she was <em>snowed under</em> with meeting notes and felt <em>overloaded</em> by constant emails.

Even small talk sounded different because the whole team was under <em>pressure</em>. During lunch, we agreed that <em>crunch time</em> before the product launch was <em>stressful</em>, and no one wanted to <em>burn out</em> before Friday.

At five o’clock, our manager reminded us to take breaks and not feel <em>overwhelmed</em> by every urgent message. We finished the most important <em>tasks</em>, shared a quick laugh near the printer, and finally decided to <em>call it a day</em>.
