## Text

Attention members: tonight’s mixed fitness session is still on, but please <em>sit tight</em> for ten minutes while we finish checking the studio floor. Once the coach confirms the equipment is safe, everyone will be <em>good to go</em>.

We want all teams to be <em>on the same page</em>: training should help you <em>blow off steam</em>, not <em>run out of steam</em> before the weekend match. If you feel exhausted, <em>take it easy</em>, drink water, and tell a staff member.

Please do not <em>cut corners</em> during warm-ups or <em>back out</em> of assigned safety checks at the last minute. We also ask captains not to <em>move the goalposts</em> after schedules are posted, as it creates confusion for volunteers.

If you are frustrated about recent changes, <em>hang in there</em> and speak to us politely rather than <em>burn bridges</em> with teammates or staff. After practice, get some rest, <em>hit the sack</em> early, and come back ready tomorrow.
