## Text

Planning a day at the <em>beach</em> is easier when you know what to expect. Choose a spot along the <em>coast</em> with clean <em>sand</em>, safe swimming areas, and enough space to relax near the <em>shore</em>.

Before you <em>swim</em> in the <em>sea</em>, check the <em>tide</em> times and look for warning flags. A strong <em>wave</em> can surprise even confident swimmers, so stay close to <em>lifeguards</em> and avoid going too far from land.

For comfort, bring <em>sunscreen</em>, water, a <em>towel</em>, and a light jacket in case a cool <em>breeze</em> arrives. You might also enjoy walking slowly along the <em>shore</em>, where you can find a smooth <em>seashell</em> or watch a <em>seagull</em> searching for food.

As the afternoon ends, many visitors stay for the <em>sunset</em> because the sky often turns orange, pink, and gold. With the sound of the <em>sea</em> in the background, a <em>beach</em> day can feel <em>peaceful</em>, simple, and memorable.
