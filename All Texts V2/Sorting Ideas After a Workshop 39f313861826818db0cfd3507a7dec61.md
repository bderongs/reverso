Subject: <em>Next Steps</em> for Organizing Workshop Ideas

Hi team,

Thanks for the lively discussion this morning. I’ve started to <em>pull together</em> the notes from the whiteboard, and my first step is to <em>categorize</em> the ideas by theme so we can see where each <em>group</em> of suggestions belongs.

By Friday, please review the shared document and <em>rank</em> the top three items you think we should focus on first. I’ll also add a quick <em>vote</em> section, so everyone has a clear way to show their <em>priorities</em> before we meet again.

Some ideas <em>overlap</em>, so we may need to <em>merge</em> similar points or <em>combine</em> smaller <em>proposals</em> into one stronger action. There is also one clear <em>split</em> between short-term fixes and longer-term projects, which we should discuss openly.

Once we <em>validate</em> the main <em>priorities</em>, I’ll <em>draft</em> a simple action plan and share it for comments. We can then <em>iterate</em> on the plan together and <em>nail down</em> <em>owners</em>, <em>deadlines</em>, and <em>next steps</em> in Monday’s meeting.

Best,

Laura
