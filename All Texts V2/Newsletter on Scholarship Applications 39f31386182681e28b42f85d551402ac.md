Subject: Important Updates for This Year’s <em>Scholarship</em> <em>Process</em>

Dear students,
We are pleased to announce that applications for the university <em>scholarship</em> program will open next Monday. This opportunity is <em>designed</em> to support students who have shown <em>significant</em> academic progress and active participation in campus or community projects.

Before applying, please review the eligibility <em>criteria</em> carefully on the student portal. You must upload each required <em>document</em>, including your transcript, personal statement, and recommendation letter, before the deadline.

A faculty <em>commission</em> will <em>evaluate</em> all applications during the first two weeks of May. The members will consider academic achievement, financial need, leadership experience, and the clarity of each applicant’s goals.

After the review, the university will <em>select</em> approximately forty recipients. Successful applicants may receive awards that <em>fund</em> part or all of their tuition for the next academic year.

If you have questions about the <em>process</em>, please contact the Financial Aid Office by email or visit us during advising hours.
