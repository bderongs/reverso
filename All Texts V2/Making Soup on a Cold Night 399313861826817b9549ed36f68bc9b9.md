I <em>seasoned</em> it with salt, black pepper, and a <em>pinch</em> of dried thyme. The smell was so <em>tasty</em> that my neighbor, who had stopped by to return a book, asked what I was <em>cooking</em>.

I used a <em>ladle</em> to fill each <em>bowl</em> and placed <em>bread</em> on the side. One <em>serve</em> was enough to <em>warm</em> us both, and I packed the <em>leftovers</em> for lunch the next day.
