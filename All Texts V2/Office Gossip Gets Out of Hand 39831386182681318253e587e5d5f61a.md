## Text

When Lena joined the marketing team, she seemed <em>friendly</em> and <em>supportive</em>. But within a month, several colleagues noticed she was <em>two-faced</em>: cheerful in meetings, yet critical of everyone afterward.

She called Mark <em>lazy</em> for leaving early to care for his sick father, which felt especially <em>hypocritical</em> because she often disappeared for long coffee breaks. Her comments became <em>spiteful</em> and <em>mean</em>, especially toward Priya, whose promotion clearly made Lena <em>jealous</em>.

At first, people dismissed the gossip as <em>petty</em> office drama. Then Lena shared a <em>dishonest</em> story about Priya losing an important client, and her <em>deceitful</em> version spread quickly through the team chat.

Her behavior grew more <em>manipulative</em> when she pretended to comfort Priya while secretly encouraging others not to trust her. Soon, even Lena’s closest work friends admitted she seemed <em>untrustworthy</em> and <em>passive-aggressive</em>.

The situation ended when a manager reviewed emails and found the <em>malicious</em> rumors had no basis. Lena received a formal warning, and the team learned how damaging character attacks can be.
