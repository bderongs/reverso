## Text

The Office of Academic Affairs announces an updated <em>framework</em> for <em>maintaining</em> integrity in all courses, effective next semester. This policy is based on the <em>principle</em> that every student’s work must be original, properly supported, and submitted with honest intent.

All written assignments will <em>require</em> a <em>citation</em> for any idea, <em>data</em>, image, or quotation taken from another <em>source</em>. <em>Plagiarism</em>, including copying from websites, sharing essays, or using AI tools without permission, is not a <em>legitimate</em> form of academic support and may lead to disciplinary action.

Students must upload each <em>major</em> <em>document</em> through the university’s verification system to <em>ensure</em> that <em>sources</em> are clearly <em>identified</em>. A <em>valid</em> student ID and course code are <em>mandatory</em> for submission, and late files without approved reasons may not be accepted.

The policy also explains students’ <em>legal</em> and academic responsibilities when using copyrighted materials. Further guidance will be provided during orientation, and instructors will answer questions in class during the first week.
