## Text

Team, this <em>brief</em> confirms that we are entering the final readiness phase for the client portal release. Since <em>inception</em>, the project has followed the approved <em>roadmap</em>, and all workstream leads are now expected to <em>hit the ground running</em> on launch preparations.

The <em>soft launch</em> will begin next Tuesday with selected enterprise users, allowing us to validate performance, onboarding, and one remaining <em>edge case</em> in billing permissions. With many <em>moving parts</em> across engineering, support, marketing, and customer success, Maya will <em>orchestrate</em> daily check-ins to keep <em>decisions</em> visible and blockers time-boxed.

Our <em>endgame</em> is a stable public release by the end of the month, provided we can <em>feasibly</em> complete security <em>sign-off</em> and training materials by Friday. Finance has also asked teams to monitor <em>burn rate</em> closely before we <em>scale up</em> paid acquisition and expand support coverage.
