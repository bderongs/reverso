Subject: A walking route for art lovers

Hi Lena,

If you have time this weekend, I suggest taking the downtown <em>sightseeing</em> route that starts near the old station. The area has a <em>lively</em> <em>atmosphere</em>, with cafés, street musicians, and plenty of <em>public art</em> to enjoy along the way.

The first thing that may <em>catch your eye</em> is a huge <em>colorful</em> <em>mural</em> on the side of the library. A few streets later, you will see a <em>minimalist</em> <em>sculpture</em> in the square, which looks especially striking against the <em>modern</em> glass buildings.

Don’t miss the <em>Art Deco</em> cinema on Park Avenue, because its <em>symmetrical</em> <em>entrance</em> is beautifully restored. Around the corner, there is also a <em>unique</em> installation made from recycled metal, and its <em>vibrant</em> colors make it a popular photo spot.

I think this route is perfect if you want <em>sightseeing</em> that feels relaxed but still inspiring. Let me know if you’d like the <em>map</em>, and I’ll send it over.

Best,
Maya
