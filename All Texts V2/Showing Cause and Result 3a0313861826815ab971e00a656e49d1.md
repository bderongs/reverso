Subject: <em>Clarifying</em> the Structure of Tomorrow’s Presentation

Hi Maya,

I reviewed your <em>slides</em> and, <em>overall</em>, the message is strong. To help the audience <em>make sense of</em> the data, start with the <em>big picture</em> before moving into the details.

The <em>key point</em> is that customer complaints increased after the software update; <em>therefore</em>, your timeline should come before the survey results. This will put the numbers in perspective and <em>thus</em> make your explanation easier to follow.

Please <em>keep in mind</em> that the managers may not know the technical background. <em>Consequently</em>, you should <em>highlight</em> the main causes and <em>underscore</em> how each one affected user experience.

The <em>bottom line</em> is that your recommendation needs to feel practical and urgent. If you connect each problem to a clear result, the presentation will sound more convincing.

Best,
Daniel
