## Text

Students packed the school hall on Tuesday to hear three candidates debate plans for the coming year. Maya Lewis appeared <em>charismatic</em> and <em>confident</em>, greeting classmates warmly before giving an <em>eloquent</em> opening speech about improving lunchtime clubs.

Her main rival, Daniel Kim, took a <em>bold</em> approach, promising a faster system for reporting problems and a more <em>decisive</em> student council. Although some listeners found his tone intense, he remained <em>dignified</em> when challenged and admitted that leaders must <em>mature</em> by accepting criticism.

The third candidate, Aisha Patel, offered a <em>sensible</em> budget plan and sounded <em>optimistic</em> about cooperation with teachers. She described herself as <em>open-minded</em> and said the council should listen to quieter students, not only the most popular voices.

By the end, many students said the debate revealed more than campaign posters ever could. The strongest candidate, they agreed, would need to be <em>humble</em> enough to learn, <em>wise</em> enough to compromise, and <em>brave</em> enough to act.
