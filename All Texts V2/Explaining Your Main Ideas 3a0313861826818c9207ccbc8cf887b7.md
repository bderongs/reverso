Subject: Tips for Tomorrow’s Client Presentation

Hi Maya,

Before tomorrow’s meeting, I wanted to <em>go over</em> the structure of your presentation. You’ll <em>cover</em> the market problem first, then <em>break down</em> our proposed solution into three simple steps.

When you introduce the data, <em>highlight</em> the growth trend and <em>point out</em> why it matters to the client. <em>In other words</em>, don’t just show the numbers; <em>clarify</em> how they connect to the client’s goals.

Please <em>emphasize</em> the cost savings in the second section, and <em>underscore</em> the fact that our plan can be implemented quickly. <em>That is</em>, <em>make clear</em> that the client will not need a long transition period or major staff changes.

At the end, <em>spell out</em> the next actions: approval, timeline, and team responsibilities. If you keep each section focused, your message will sound confident, practical, and easy to follow.

Best,
Daniel
