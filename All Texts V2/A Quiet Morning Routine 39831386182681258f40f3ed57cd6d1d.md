## Text

Many people begin the day by reaching for their phones, but a slower routine can create a <em>calm</em> start. Opening a window, making tea, and sitting in silence for five minutes can help the body feel <em>relaxed</em> and the mind become more <em>peaceful</em>.

Small habits also build <em>contentment</em>. A simple breakfast, a short walk, or a few notes in a journal can leave you <em>satisfied</em> before work even begins, and on difficult mornings these rituals may cheer you up.

When plans change or traffic is heavy, it is easier to <em>keep your cool</em> if the morning has already offered a little <em>bliss</em>. People often feel <em>relieved</em> when they are not rushing, and they may feel <em>comforted</em> by familiar sounds, such as a kettle boiling or birds outside.

Starting the day <em>in good spirits</em> does not require expensive tools or a perfect schedule. It only takes attention to what makes you feel <em>glad</em>, steady, and ready for the hours ahead.
