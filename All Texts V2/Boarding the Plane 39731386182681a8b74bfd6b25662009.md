## Text

Good afternoon, passengers. We are now ready to <em>board</em> <em>Flight</em> 482 to Denver at <em>gate</em> 16, beginning with families <em>traveling</em> with small children and passengers needing extra assistance.

Please have your <em>boarding pass</em> and photo identification ready for our agents. After priority groups, we will invite passengers by <em>row</em> number, starting from the rear of the aircraft.

When you enter the <em>cabin</em>, please move quickly to your assigned <em>seat</em>. If you have an <em>aisle seat</em> or a <em>window seat</em>, step into your <em>row</em> carefully and allow others to pass through the <em>aisle</em>.

Place your <em>carry-on</em> in the <em>overhead compartment</em> above your <em>seat</em>, and <em>stow</em> smaller personal items under the <em>seat</em> in front of you. A <em>flight attendant</em> will be available to help if the compartment is full or if you need assistance.

Once seated, fasten your <em>seat belt</em> and keep the <em>aisle</em> clear. Thank you for your cooperation, and we wish you a pleasant <em>flight</em>.
