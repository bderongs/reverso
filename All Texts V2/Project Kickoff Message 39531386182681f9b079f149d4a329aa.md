Hi team,

I hope you all had a good <em>weekend</em>. I’d like to <em>get the ball rolling</em> on our new marketing <em>project</em> and make sure everyone feels ready to <em>hit the ground running</em>.

One piece of <em>food for thought</em>: a short customer survey could be a real <em>game-changer</em> for understanding what the audience wants. Thanks in advance for your energy and input.

Best,
Laura
