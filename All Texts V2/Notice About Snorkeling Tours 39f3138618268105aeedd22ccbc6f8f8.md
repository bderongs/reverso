## Text

Guests are advised that daily snorkeling tours will depart from the main <em>pier</em> at 9:00 a.m. and 2:00 p.m., weather permitting. Each tour includes a short trip by <em>sailboat</em> across the <em>bay</em> to a protected <em>reef</em> known for its <em>crystal clear</em> water and bright turquoise shallows.

Please bring a <em>towel</em>, <em>sunscreen</em>, and a <em>waterproof</em> bag for personal items. A <em>snorkel</em>, mask, and fins will be provided, and a certified <em>lifeguard</em> will give safety instructions before anyone enters the water.

Participants may see <em>coral</em>, <em>starfish</em>, small tropical fish, and patches of <em>seaweed</em> during the guided swim. Do not touch marine life, and never <em>dive</em> near the <em>reef</em>, as the <em>coral</em> is fragile and easily damaged.

Occasionally, <em>jellyfish</em> may appear after strong winds or changing <em>tides</em>. If this happens, the tour may be delayed, moved to another part of the <em>bay</em>, or cancelled for safety.
