Subject: About what happened yesterday

Hi Maya,

I’ve been thinking about our conversation, and I want to be honest with you. When I found out you had told Lena about my problem, it felt like a real <em>betrayal</em> of <em>trust</em>, and I couldn’t hide my <em>disappointment</em>.

Maybe there was a <em>misunderstanding</em>, but it was still a painful <em>letdown</em> because I believed our talks were private. I don’t want to <em>hold a grudge</em>, yet I need some <em>reassurance</em> that my feelings matter to you.

I know <em>friendships</em> are not perfect, and people make mistakes. If you can <em>apologize</em> sincerely, I think I’ll be able to <em>forgive</em> you, but <em>honesty</em> has to come first.

I hope we can talk calmly and <em>make up</em> soon. Our <em>friendship</em> means a lot to me, and I don’t want one bad moment to ruin everything.

Take care,
Sophie
