## Text

The <em>break room</em> is more than a place to grab a <em>snack</em>; it is where new staff often get to know the rhythm of the office. If you need a coffee <em>refill</em> or a quiet minute, feel free to use the space between meetings.

Most <em>folks</em> <em>take a break</em> there in the late morning or after lunch, when the office has a little <em>downtime</em>. The mood is usually <em>low-key</em>, with light <em>banter</em> about <em>weekend</em> <em>plans</em>, the weather, or a new <em>project</em>.

A bit of <em>chit-chat</em> can help you build friendly connections, but avoid <em>gossip</em> or comments that might make others uncomfortable. If a conversation turns too personal, it is perfectly fine to smile and step away.

When you have time, <em>stick around</em> for a few minutes instead of rushing back to your desk. Short, friendly conversations can help you <em>recharge</em> and feel more at home at work.
