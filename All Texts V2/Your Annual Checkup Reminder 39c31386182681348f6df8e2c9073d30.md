Subject: Time to <em>Schedule</em> Your Routine Visit

Dear Ms. Carter,

We hope you are in good <em>health</em>. Our records show that you are due for your annual <em>checkup</em> with Dr. Lewis, which includes a general <em>examination</em> and an update to your <em>medical record</em>.

During your visit, the nurse will measure your <em>vital signs</em>, including <em>blood pressure</em>, <em>pulse</em>, and <em>temperature</em>. Depending on your age and <em>health</em> history, Dr. Lewis may also recommend a <em>screening</em> or additional <em>blood work</em>.

If <em>lab work</em> is needed, you may be asked to fast before your <em>appointment</em>. The results usually arrive within a few days, and our office will contact you if a <em>follow-up</em> is necessary.

Please call us at 555-0198 or use the patient portal to choose a convenient time. We look forward to helping you stay well.

Kind regards,
Green Valley Family <em>Clinic</em>
