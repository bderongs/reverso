Subject: <em>Menu</em> Questions for Friday’s <em>Reservation</em>

Dear Bistro Garden Team,

I’m writing about our <em>table</em> for six this Friday at 7:30 p.m. Before we arrive, I’d like to check a few dietary needs, as one guest has a severe <em>peanut allergy</em> and another has a gluten <em>intolerance</em>.

Could you let me know which <em>starters</em> are <em>dairy-free</em>? One friend is <em>lactose intolerant</em>, and we want to avoid any <em>dishes</em> made with cream, butter, or cheese.

We will also have one <em>vegan</em> guest and one <em>vegetarian</em> guest, so it would be helpful to know whether the mushroom risotto or vegetable curry can be prepared without animal products. Do you offer any halal or kosher options on your regular <em>menu</em>?

Finally, could you <em>recommend</em> a <em>sugar-free</em> <em>dessert</em> or fruit <em>plate</em>? Some of us enjoy <em>spicy</em> food, but others prefer something <em>mild</em>, so please mention the heat level of your sauces.

Thank you for your help. We’re looking forward to dining with you.

Best regards,
Elena Martin
