Last Friday, I came home late but still wanted something <em>fresh</em> for dinner. I <em>rinsed</em> two fillets, patted them <em>dry</em>, and decided to <em>sear</em> one in a hot <em>frying pan</em> while I let the other <em>poach</em> gently in lemon water.

While that <em>cooked</em>, I put green beans in a small <em>basket</em> to <em>steam</em> over the <em>pot</em>. The poached fillet stayed <em>moist</em> and delicate, so I checked it with a <em>fork</em> to make sure it flaked easily.

For my brother, who loves <em>stronger</em> flavors, I chose to <em>fry</em> a few potato slices until golden. We ate at the counter, comparing the <em>tender</em> fish, the <em>crunchy</em> potatoes, and the clean taste of the vegetables.
