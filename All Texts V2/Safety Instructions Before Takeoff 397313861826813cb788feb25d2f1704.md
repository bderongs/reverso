## Text

Good morning, ladies and gentlemen. On behalf of the <em>captain</em> and the entire <em>crew</em>, welcome aboard, and please give us your attention as we prepare for <em>takeoff</em>.

Please <em>buckle up</em> now and make sure your <em>seat belt</em> is fastened low and tight across your lap. Your <em>seat</em> back should be straight, your <em>tray table</em> secured, and you should sit <em>upright</em> until the <em>seat belt</em> sign has been switched off.

In the unlikely event of a loss of <em>cabin</em> pressure, an <em>oxygen mask</em> will drop from the panel above you. Pull the mask toward you, place it over your nose and mouth, and breathe normally before helping others.

A <em>life vest</em> is located under your <em>seat</em>, and instructions for use are shown on the safety card in the <em>seat</em> pocket. Please also note the nearest <em>emergency exit</em>, which may be behind you.

If we need to <em>evacuate</em> the aircraft, leave all personal belongings behind and follow the crew’s instructions. The <em>cockpit</em> will remain in communication with the <em>cabin</em> throughout the <em>flight</em>.
