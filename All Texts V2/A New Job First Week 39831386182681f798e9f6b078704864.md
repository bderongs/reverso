## Text

On Monday, I started at a small marketing company and tried to <em>learn the ropes</em> without looking too nervous. My manager told me to follow procedures <em>by the book</em> at first, then I could suggest better ways to <em>work out</em> problems.

By Wednesday, I began to <em>get the hang of</em> the software, although I did <em>run into</em> a few confusing reports. A kind teammate <em>bent over backward</em> to help me <em>catch up</em>, even showing me how the client system had been built <em>from scratch</em>.

The company was created <em>from the ground up</em> by two friends who learned business in the <em>school of hard knocks</em>. They expect everyone to <em>go the extra mile</em>, but they also make sure new employees feel <em>on the right track</em>.
