## Text

Effective 1 August, all vendors must submit updated insurance certificates, data-protection contacts, and tax forms through the supplier portal. We know this adds some <em>red tape</em>, but the new process helps us <em>cover all bases</em> before contract renewals.

Please complete each field <em>by the book</em> and upload supporting documents where requested. As a <em>rule of thumb</em>, if a requirement seems like a <em>gray area</em>, ask Procurement before submitting; missing ownership details or expired licenses will be treated as a <em>red flag</em>.

Our review team will <em>leave no stone unturned</em>, so files must <em>pass muster</em> before any purchase orders are released. The current checklist is not <em>set in stone</em>, and we will keep all registered contacts <em>in the loop</em> if regulations change.

A short training video is available to bring your team <em>up to speed</em>. Thank you for your cooperation and for helping us maintain a reliable, compliant supplier network.
