Subject: Key Takeaways from Today’s Kickoff

Hi Team,

Thanks for joining today’s kickoff meeting. I’m glad we are <em>on the same page</em> about the <em>big picture</em>: launching the client portal by the end of Q2 while keeping quality high.

The <em>bottom line</em> is that each department will need to <em>wear many hats</em> during the first phase. Marketing will bring customer insights to the table, while IT will help newer team members <em>learn the ropes</em> and get <em>up to speed</em> quickly.

Please keep me <em>in the loop</em> on any risks, delays, or budget concerns. To <em>cover all bases</em>, we’ll review progress every Friday and confirm that legal, design, and support are aligned.

Sarah will <em>call the shots</em> on final feature priorities, but everyone’s input is welcome. If we all <em>go the extra mile</em> and come <em>on board</em> with clear communication, we can deliver a strong product on time.

Best,
Michael
