Subject: Request for <em>Decision</em> on the Customer Portal Project

Hi Maria,

I’m writing to ask for the <em>green light</em> to move the customer portal project into the planning phase. The <em>leadership</em> team’s <em>vision</em> for improving client experience is clear, and this proposal supports that <em>strategy</em> by reducing response times and giving customers better visibility.

Over the past two weeks, I met with Sales, Support, and IT to <em>align</em> <em>priorities</em> and confirm <em>resource</em> needs. There is strong <em>alignment</em> around the main benefits, but we still need your <em>influence</em> to <em>persuade</em> Finance that the initial investment is justified.

If approved, each department will <em>commit</em> one representative to the project team for the first eight weeks. I will be <em>accountable</em> for the delivery <em>schedule</em>, <em>risk</em> log, and weekly status updates, while department leads will maintain <em>accountability</em> for their <em>assigned</em> <em>tasks</em>.

Your <em>commitment</em> would help us move forward with confidence and avoid <em>delays</em> in Q3. Please let me know if you would like a short briefing before making a <em>decision</em>.

Best regards,
Daniel
