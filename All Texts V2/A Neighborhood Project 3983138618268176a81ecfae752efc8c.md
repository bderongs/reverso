Subject: Saturday’s Community Garden Meeting

Hi Maya,

Thanks for helping <em>behind the scenes</em> with the community garden plan. I know we’re all <em>in the same boat</em>, trying to balance work, family, and this project.

At the meeting, we didn’t always <em>see eye to eye</em>, especially about the budget. I tried to <em>play devil’s advocate</em> when Tom suggested expensive lights, but I think that was a <em>red herring</em> because the real <em>elephant in the room</em> is who will maintain the garden after summer.

Some neighbors want to <em>think outside the box</em> and ask local cafés for food-waste compost. That idea may <em>go over some people’s heads</em> at first, and getting approval will be <em>easier said than done</em>.

Still, we should <em>give it a go</em>. <em>At the end of the day</em>, doing nothing feels like a <em>slippery slope</em> toward losing the space completely.

Best,

Daniel
