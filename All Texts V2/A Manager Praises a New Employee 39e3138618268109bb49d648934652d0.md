Subject: Excellent Progress in Your First Month

Hi Daniel,

I wanted to take a moment to recognize the strong impression you have made since joining our team. You are already proving to be a real <em>asset</em>, and your <em>diligence</em> during training has helped you become a <em>fast learner</em> in a very short time.

What <em>stands out</em> most is your <em>proactive</em> attitude and your willingness to <em>take the initiative</em> when a task needs attention. Whether you are preparing client notes, asking thoughtful questions, or offering support to colleagues, you show that you are <em>focused</em>, <em>motivated</em>, and <em>dedicated</em> to doing the job well.

Several team members have mentioned that you are a <em>go-getter</em> with a <em>consistent</em> approach to your work. If you continue at this pace, I am confident you will <em>impress</em> even more people across the company.

Best regards,

Maria Lewis
Operations Manager
