## Text

Healthy <em>relationships</em> can have disagreements, but they should not make you feel afraid, small, or constantly confused. Early warning signs may include <em>love bombing</em>, where someone overwhelms you with attention, gifts, and <em>promises</em> before real <em>trust</em> has formed.

A partner who is <em>controlling</em> may check your phone, question your <em>friendships</em>, or act like a <em>possessive</em> who has the right to decide where you go. <em>Jealousy</em> and <em>insecurity</em> are normal feelings sometimes, but they become dangerous when used to <em>intimidate</em> you or limit your freedom.

Emotional <em>abuse</em> can be subtle. Someone may <em>gaslight</em> you by denying things they clearly said, <em>humiliate</em> you in front of others, or use stonewalling as a <em>stonewall</em> of silence to punish you after an argument.

<em>Neglect</em> is another sign, especially when your needs are ignored and you are <em>taken for granted</em>. If a partner repeatedly lies, <em>cheat on</em> you, or blames you for their behavior, it may be time to seek <em>support</em> from someone you trust.
