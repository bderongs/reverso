We are pleased to announce a special family day this Saturday at our popular <em>zoo</em> and <em>aquarium</em>, a favorite <em>tourist attraction</em> for visitors of all ages. Gates open at 9:00 a.m., and the main <em>entrance</em> will have staff available to answer questions and provide each family with a free <em>map</em>.

A <em>guided tour</em> will begin at 10:30 a.m., featuring the new rainforest <em>exhibition</em>, playful monkeys, <em>colorful</em> fish, and <em>beautiful</em> sea turtles. The <em>atmosphere</em> is expected to be <em>lively</em>, so guests are encouraged to arrive early, especially as the site may become <em>crowded</em> by midday.

Families are welcome to bring lunch and relax in the shaded <em>picnic area</em> near the children’s playground. Free spaces are available in the <em>parking lot</em> until full, and additional public transport information can be found on our website.
