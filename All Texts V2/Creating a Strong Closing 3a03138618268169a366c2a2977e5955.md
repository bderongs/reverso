Subject: Tips for Ending Your Presentation Effectively

Hi Maya,

I reviewed your draft <em>slides</em>, and <em>overall</em>, the structure is clear and persuasive. To <em>wrap up</em> the presentation, make sure your final minute feels planned rather than rushed.

Start your <em>wrap-up</em> with a brief <em>recap</em> of the three main points, then offer a short <em>summary</em> of why they matter. You can say, “<em>In summary</em>, our proposal reduces costs, improves teamwork, and supports long-term growth.”

After that, move toward your <em>conclusion</em> with confidence. <em>In conclusion</em>, avoid adding new data; <em>instead</em>, <em>sum up</em> the value of your message in simple, memorable language.

<em>Finally</em>, your <em>closing</em> should include a strong <em>call to action</em>. Ask the audience to approve the next step, schedule a follow-up meeting, or review the proposal by Friday.

Best,
Daniel
