Subject: Quick plan for Sunday cooking

Hi Maya,

I’m going to <em>prepare</em> a big <em>batch</em> of lentil soup on Sunday, plus some roasted vegetables and rice. If you’re free, come over around 3 p.m. and we can divide everything into a <em>container</em> for each day.

After the food <em>cools</em>, we’ll <em>seal</em> the <em>lids</em> tightly so nothing <em>spills</em> in the <em>fridge</em>. I’ll <em>put away</em> the <em>fresh</em> herbs in the top <em>drawer</em> and <em>store away</em> the extra onions and potatoes in the <em>pantry</em>.

Could you clear one <em>shelf</em> in the <em>fridge</em> before you come? We’ll keep Monday and Tuesday meals there, and <em>freeze</em> the rest for later in the week.

For dinner, we can <em>heat up</em> any <em>leftovers</em> from Friday instead of <em>cooking</em> again. I’ll also label each <em>container</em>, so we don’t forget what’s inside.

See you Sunday,
Nina
