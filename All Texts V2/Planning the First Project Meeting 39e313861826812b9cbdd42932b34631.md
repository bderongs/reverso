Subject: Preparing to <em>Kick Off</em> the Client Portal Project

Hi team,

I’d like to <em>kick off</em> the client portal project next Tuesday at 10:00 a.m. Please review the attached <em>agenda</em> before the meeting so we can <em>focus on</em> the main <em>objective</em>: agreeing on <em>priorities</em> for the first phase.

During the call, we will confirm the overall <em>schedule</em>, discuss the project <em>timeline</em>, and identify the first major <em>milestone</em>. We also need to agree on the final <em>deadline</em> for the initial <em>deliverable</em>, which is the working prototype for internal testing.

Before we meet, please check whether your department has the necessary <em>resource</em> capacity and whether any <em>budget</em> concerns should be raised early. I will <em>assign</em> each workstream during the meeting, but I’d like everyone to think about which <em>responsibility</em> best fits their expertise.

Thanks in advance for coming prepared. If you have any questions or suggested additions to the <em>agenda</em>, please send them by Friday.

Best regards,

Maya
