## Text

Announcement: Employees returning after <em>illness</em> or <em>injury</em> must submit a medical note to Human Resources before resuming regular duties. If you have been <em>under the weather</em> but are now <em>on the mend</em>, your doctor should state whether you are <em>back on your feet</em> and ready for normal work.

The note should confirm that you have received a <em>clean bill of health</em> or explain any <em>temporary</em> limits. For example, staff with <em>impaired</em> mobility, a short-term <em>disability</em>, or <em>instructions</em> to remain <em>confined</em> to light duties may need adjusted tasks.

Managers will follow medical <em>guidance</em> and make reasonable workplace arrangements where possible. Employees must <em>comply</em> with the recommended restrictions, such as <em>avoiding</em> heavy lifting, taking extra breaks, or <em>cutting back</em> on overtime until they are <em>fit as a fiddle</em> again.
