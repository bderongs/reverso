Hi Maya,

I know everyone is working <em>under pressure</em>, especially now that we are in a serious <em>time crunch</em>. During <em>crunch time</em>, we can <em>save time</em> by holding a 15-minute daily check-in instead of long status meetings.

If you agree, I will <em>update</em> the <em>timeline</em> today and share the revised <em>deadline</em> with the client by 4 p.m.

Best,
Daniel
