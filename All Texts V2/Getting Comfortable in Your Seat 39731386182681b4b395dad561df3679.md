## Text

A little preparation can make an economy <em>flight</em> feel much easier, especially on a <em>long haul</em>. Before <em>takeoff</em>, adjust the <em>headrest</em>, check that your <em>seat belt</em> is fastened, and place small essentials where you can reach them without disturbing your neighbor.

Once the <em>crew</em> allows it, you may <em>recline</em> your <em>seat</em> slightly, but do it slowly and consider the passenger behind you. Keep your bag under the <em>seat</em> only if it does not reduce your <em>legroom</em>, and remember that the <em>armrest</em> is often shared space.

For <em>meals</em> or work, lower the <em>tray table</em> carefully and keep drinks away from electronics. A good <em>headphone</em> can make films and <em>announcements</em> clearer, while a <em>neck pillow</em> supports your head during rest.

When the <em>cabin</em> lights dim, a <em>blanket</em> and an <em>eye mask</em> can help you sleep more comfortably. During <em>landing</em>, return your <em>seat</em> to the upright position and make sure the <em>tray table</em> is locked.
