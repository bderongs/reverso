Instead of treating me like just another <em>acquaintance</em>, she invited me to join her table, and we quickly <em>hit it off</em> over our <em>love</em> of travel and terrible office coffee.

Over the next few weeks, a real <em>bond</em> grew between us. She helped me <em>feel at home</em> at work, and even after she moved to another branch, we promised to <em>keep in touch</em> and <em>catch up</em> every Friday after work.
