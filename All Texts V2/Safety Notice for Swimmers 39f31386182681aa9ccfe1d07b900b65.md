## Text

Attention <em>beach</em> visitors: conditions are changing this afternoon as the <em>tide</em> comes in. A strong <em>wave</em> pattern has been reported near the rocks, and a <em>riptide</em> may form beyond the marked swimming area.

Please <em>swim</em> only between the yellow flags and follow all instructions from the <em>lifeguard</em> on duty. Do not <em>dive</em> in shallow water, and if you feel tired, turn onto your back and <em>float</em> while raising one arm for help.

<em>Jellyfish</em> have been seen close to <em>shore</em>, so avoid touching any <em>sea</em> creatures, even if they appear harmless. The <em>beach</em> is <em>crowded</em> today, so keep children within sight and store phones and valuables in a <em>waterproof</em> bag.

To prevent <em>sunburn</em>, apply <em>sunscreen</em> regularly and wear a <em>hat</em> during the hottest hours. Thank you for helping us keep this <em>beach</em> safe and enjoyable for everyone.
