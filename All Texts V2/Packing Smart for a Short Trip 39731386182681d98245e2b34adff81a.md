Subject: What to Bring for Your Weekend <em>Flight</em>

Hi Maya,

Since our <em>trip</em> is only two nights, I really think we should <em>travel light</em>. I’m going to <em>pack</em> just one <em>carry-on</em>, and I’ll bring a small <em>backpack</em> as my personal item.

You probably don’t need a large <em>suitcase</em> unless you want extra space for shopping. The <em>airline</em> allows one piece of <em>hand luggage</em>, but they may ask us to drop it off at the <em>gate</em> if the <em>flight</em> is full.

For the plane, I’m taking my <em>neck pillow</em>, an <em>eye mask</em>, and a thin <em>blanket</em> because the <em>cabin</em> can get cold. I’ll also download a few shows on my <em>tablet</em> so I don’t have to rely on the in-flight entertainment.

Try to choose clothes that match easily, and wear your heaviest shoes to save space. Let me know if you need help checking the <em>baggage</em> rules before we leave.

See you Friday,
Anna
