## Text

Please remember that our beginner bakery class starts this Saturday at 10:00 a.m. in the community center kitchen. Bring a clean <em>apron</em>, and arrive ten minutes early so we can check everyone in and review safety rules.

During the lesson, you will learn how to <em>sift</em> flour, <em>knead</em> <em>dough</em>, and <em>roll</em> pastry evenly with a <em>rolling pin</em>. We will also use a <em>whisk</em>, a <em>mixer</em>, and a <em>kitchen scale</em> to <em>measure</em> <em>ingredients</em> accurately and <em>prepare</em> a simple <em>cake</em> batter.

All basic <em>ingredients</em> will be provided, including flour, sugar, butter, and eggs. Each student will receive a <em>cake pan</em> and will practice filling a <em>pastry bag</em> for decorating.

Please tie back long hair, wear closed-toe shoes, and avoid <em>strong</em> perfume in the kitchen. If you have any food allergies or need to cancel your place, contact the bakery office by Thursday afternoon.
