## Text

A well-equipped hotel room is designed to make a guest’s <em>stay</em> comfortable, practical, and relaxing. Near the entrance, you will often find a <em>closet</em> with several <em>hangers</em> for coats, dresses, or travel clothes.

Most rooms include a <em>desk</em> and a <em>chair</em>, giving business travelers a place to work or write notes. A large <em>mirror</em> is usually placed near the bathroom or wardrobe, helping guests get ready before leaving for meetings, dinners, or sightseeing.

For convenience, many hotels provide a <em>minibar</em> stocked with drinks and snacks, though prices are usually higher than in local shops. A small <em>safe</em> is also common, allowing guests to store passports, cash, and valuable items securely.

Temperature control is another important feature: an <em>air conditioner</em> keeps the room cool in summer, while a <em>heater</em> makes it warm during colder months. Some rooms also have a <em>balcony</em>, where guests can enjoy fresh air and a pleasant <em>view</em> of the city, garden, or sea.
