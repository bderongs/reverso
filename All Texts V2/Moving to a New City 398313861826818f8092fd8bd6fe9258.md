Subject: First week in my new apartment

Hi Maya,

I’ve been here for six days, and my emotions keep changing. Some mornings I wake up feeling <em>hopeful</em> and even <em>optimistic</em>, especially when sunlight fills the kitchen.

By evening, though, I sometimes feel <em>homesick</em> and <em>lonely</em>. The streets are busy, but I still feel <em>out of place</em>, like everyone else understands a rhythm I haven’t learned yet.

Yesterday I passed a bakery that smelled like the one near our old school, and I became strangely <em>nostalgic</em>. It made me a little <em>melancholic</em> and <em>sad</em>, and I walked home <em>downcast</em> in the rain.

Starting a new job also makes me <em>uneasy</em>. Everyone is polite, but being the newest person leaves me feeling <em>vulnerable</em>, as if one small mistake will reveal too much.

Still, I felt <em>comforted</em> after my neighbor invited me for tea and told me she felt the same way when she arrived. I think I’ll be okay; I just need time.

Love,
Anna
