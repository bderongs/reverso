Subject: You must visit this amazing <em>viewpoint</em>

Hi Clara,

I finally went up the old stone <em>tower</em> yesterday, and I thought of you immediately. The climb was a little tiring, but the <em>observation deck</em> at the top was worth every step.

From there, you can <em>look out over</em> the whole city and <em>take in</em> a truly <em>breathtaking</em> <em>panorama</em>. The <em>skyline</em> looked especially <em>beautiful</em> at sunset, with church <em>spires</em>, glass offices, and <em>bridges</em> glowing against the <em>horizon</em>.

There is also a small <em>overlook</em> on the north side, which gives a quieter <em>view</em> of the <em>river</em> and the older neighborhoods. I loved how the <em>cityscape</em> changed from narrow streets nearby to green hills in the distance.

If you come next month, we should go together in the late afternoon. It is a <em>spectacular</em> spot for photos, and there is a café nearby where we can rest afterward.

Best,
Marta
