Subject: Saturday Cleanup on the North <em>Coast</em>

Dear volunteers,

Thank you for joining our <em>beach</em> cleanup this Saturday. We will meet at 8:30 a.m. near the wooden steps leading down to the <em>shore</em>, where the <em>sand</em> often collects plastic after high <em>tide</em>.

Our team will walk along the <em>coast</em> and sort litter into recycling bags. Please be careful around piles of <em>seaweed</em>, as a small <em>crab</em> or a fragile <em>seashell</em> may be hidden underneath.

This area is close to a protected <em>coral</em> <em>reef</em>, so keeping waste out of the <em>ocean</em> is especially important. A <em>pristine</em> coastline helps fish, seabirds, and every <em>seagull</em> that feeds near the water.

Bring gloves, a reusable bottle, and sun protection. After the cleanup, we will record what we found and share the results with the local marine conservation office.

Best regards,

Maya
<em>Beach</em> Cleanup Coordinator
