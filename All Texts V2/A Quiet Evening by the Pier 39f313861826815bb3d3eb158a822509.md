## Text

After work, Lena walked down to the old wooden <em>pier</em> with a sandwich in her bag and <em>sand</em> still warm under her shoes. The <em>sea</em> was calm, and a light <em>breeze</em> moved through the leaves of a tall <em>palm tree</em> near the <em>shore</em>.

She sat on a bench and watched a small <em>sailboat</em> cross the <em>turquoise</em> of the <em>bay</em>. Every <em>wave</em> rolled in softly, spreading a thin line of foam over the <em>crystal clear</em> water.

A <em>seagull</em> landed beside her, hoping for crumbs, but Lena only smiled and kept her dinner wrapped. As the <em>sunset</em> turned the clouds orange and pink, the <em>beach</em> grew quieter, and families began packing their <em>towels</em>.

For a while, she listened to the gentle sounds around her and forgot about emails, traffic, and deadlines. The evening felt <em>peaceful</em>, as if the whole <em>coast</em> had slowed down just for her.
