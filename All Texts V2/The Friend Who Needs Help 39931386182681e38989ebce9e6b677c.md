Subject: I’m worried about Maya

Hi Daniel,

I wanted to ask your advice about Maya. Since she joined our class, some people have treated her like an <em>outcast</em>, and a few even <em>shun</em> her at lunch. I think it has become <em>bullying</em>, not just <em>teasing</em>.

Yesterday, I saw Liam mock her accent, and she looked <em>close</em> to tears. I tried to <em>stand up to</em> him, but I felt nervous and didn’t say enough. Maya needs an <em>ally</em>, and I want to show more <em>compassion</em>.

She told me she doesn’t feel <em>cared-for</em>, <em>loved</em>, or <em>appreciated</em> here, and that she can’t <em>fit in</em> no matter how hard she tries. I <em>care</em> about her because she is <em>funny</em>, thoughtful, and honestly a <em>kindred spirit</em>. Maybe what she needs most is <em>reassurance</em> that she is not alone.

Could we sit with her tomorrow and invite her to the study group? I think a small gesture could make a real difference.

Best,
Sofia
