## Text

This Saturday and Sunday, the city <em>museum</em> invites visitors to explore a new <em>exhibition</em> in the north <em>wing</em>, featuring rare objects from its permanent <em>collection</em>. The main <em>hall</em> will display a restored <em>masterpiece</em> by Elena Marquez, alongside a vivid <em>painting</em> recently donated by a local family.

In the <em>modern</em> <em>art gallery</em>, guests can view bold <em>artwork</em> by emerging artists, including a metal <em>sculpture</em> inspired by the city <em>skyline</em>. A special display will also present an ancient <em>artifact</em> discovered during last year’s riverfront excavation.

A <em>guided tour</em> begins every hour from the information desk, offering stories behind the artists, materials, and historical context. Visitors who prefer to explore independently may borrow an <em>audio guide</em> at the <em>entrance</em> for a small fee.

Family activities run from 11 a.m. to 3 p.m., and the café will serve weekend refreshments. Admission is free before noon, but advance booking is recommended due to limited space.
