In the café downstairs, we <em>struck up a conversation</em> about the <em>weekend</em> and discovered a funny <em>coincidence</em>: we had both gone to the same tiny Italian restaurant on Saturday night. She laughed and said her usual <em>partner in crime</em> for food adventures had canceled, so she went alone.

Back at the office, the usual <em>buzz</em> had started, but we let our chat <em>linger</em> for a few minutes. We avoided <em>gossip</em> and instead shared harmless office habits, like my <em>pet peeve</em> about people leaving empty milk cartons in the fridge.

Lina said she hated making a <em>fuss</em>, but she might put up a polite note someday. Before we returned to our desks, we agreed to <em>catch up</em> again next week.
