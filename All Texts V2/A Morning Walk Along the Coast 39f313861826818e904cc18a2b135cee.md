## Text

Yesterday, I woke up early and walked along the <em>coast</em> before the town became busy. A cool <em>breeze</em> came from the <em>sea</em>, and each <em>wave</em> rolled gently onto the <em>shore</em>, leaving smooth patterns in the wet <em>sand</em>.

Near a quiet pool of water, I noticed a small <em>crab</em> moving sideways under some <em>seaweed</em>. A bright orange <em>starfish</em> rested on a rock, and I picked up a striped <em>seashell</em> that looked almost polished by the water.

Farther down the <em>beach</em>, a <em>seagull</em> circled above me, calling loudly as if it owned the whole place. I stopped beside a tall <em>palm tree</em> and watched fishermen prepare their boats while children built castles in the <em>sand</em>.

By the time I returned in the evening, the sky was glowing with a soft <em>sunset</em>. The same <em>shore</em> felt different then—calmer, warmer, and full of the <em>peaceful</em> sound of the <em>sea</em>.
