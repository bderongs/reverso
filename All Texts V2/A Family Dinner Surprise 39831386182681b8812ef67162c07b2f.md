## Text

Last Friday, my sister asked me to help plan a secret birthday dinner for Dad. She said we had to <em>keep it under wraps</em>, because if <em>word gets around</em> in our family, someone will <em>spill the beans</em> by accident.

At lunch, Mom noticed us whispering and asked what was going on. I smiled and said, “<em>Mum’s the word</em>,” but then I pretended the party was for our cat, just to <em>pull her leg</em>.

By evening, Dad said he felt a bit <em>under the weather</em> and almost stayed home. Bringing soup first was a <em>good call</em>, because it helped him feel better and really <em>made a difference</em>.

When he walked into the restaurant and saw everyone, he was <em>on cloud nine</em>. The cake was a <em>piece of cake</em> to arrange, and the old family photos on the table were the <em>cherry on top</em>.

Dad hugged us and said our message was received <em>loud and clear</em>: family time matters most. Nobody regretted the effort, especially when he laughed until he cried.
