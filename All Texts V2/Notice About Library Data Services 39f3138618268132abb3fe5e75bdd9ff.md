Beginning Monday, the university library will <em>expand</em> its support for students using academic <em>data</em> in research projects. A new online <em>section</em> of the library website will list each <em>available</em> <em>source</em>, including government reports, <em>journal</em> archives, and statistical collections.

Students who need to <em>obtain</em> access to restricted databases must add their names to the <em>register</em> at the reference desk or complete the digital request form. Please include the <em>specific</em> course, assignment, or research topic so that librarians can <em>identify</em> the most useful materials.

For projects <em>requiring</em> <em>technical</em> guidance, trained staff will <em>assist</em> with searching databases, downloading files, and citing digital <em>resources</em> correctly. Short workshops will be offered every Wednesday afternoon, and all registered participants will receive a guide to responsible <em>data</em> use.
