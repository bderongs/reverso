## Text

When temperatures rise, the right <em>fabric</em> can make the difference between feeling fresh and feeling uncomfortable. <em>Cotton</em> is a popular choice because it is <em>soft</em>, <em>breathable</em>, and easy to wash, making it ideal for T-shirts, <em>casual</em> <em>dresses</em>, and everyday <em>shirts</em>.

<em>Linen</em> is another summer favorite, especially for trousers, <em>blouses</em>, and relaxed <em>suits</em>. It is <em>lightweight</em> and <em>breathable</em>, although it wrinkles easily, which many shoppers accept as part of its <em>natural</em> charm.

For <em>dressier</em> occasions, <em>silk</em> offers a <em>smooth</em> feel and an elegant look, but it is a <em>delicate</em> material that often needs careful washing or dry cleaning. Some <em>silk</em> tops may also be slightly <em>sheer</em>, so it is worth checking them in bright light before buying.

<em>Polyester</em> and <em>nylon</em> are common in modern clothing, particularly sportswear and travel <em>outfits</em>. They can be <em>durable</em> and quick-drying, but they may feel less <em>breathable</em> than <em>natural</em> materials, so always check the <em>label</em> and try <em>items</em> on before deciding.
