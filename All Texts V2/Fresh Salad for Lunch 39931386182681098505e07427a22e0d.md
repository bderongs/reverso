## Text

A bright homemade salad can turn a simple lunch into something colorful and satisfying. Start by placing a clean <em>cutting board</em> on the counter and using a sharp <em>knife</em> to <em>slice</em> cucumbers, tomatoes, and red onions into even pieces.

For extra texture, <em>chop</em> a <em>handful</em> of lettuce and <em>grate</em> a carrot with a box <em>grater</em>. If the cucumber skin feels tough, use a <em>peeler</em> before you <em>cut</em> it, then put the vegetables in a <em>colander</em> to <em>rinse</em> them under cold water and <em>drain</em> well.

Transfer everything to a large <em>bowl</em> and <em>toss</em> the vegetables gently with olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and pepper. <em>Sprinkle</em> a few toasted seeds on top for a <em>crunchy</em> finish, and add a <em>zesty</em> dressing with mustard or vinegar if you want a <em>stronger</em> flavor.

Serve the salad immediately with <em>bread</em>, grilled chicken, or cheese for a balanced meal. Keeping the <em>ingredients</em> <em>fresh</em> and well-prepared makes each bite taste clean, crisp, and full of flavor.
