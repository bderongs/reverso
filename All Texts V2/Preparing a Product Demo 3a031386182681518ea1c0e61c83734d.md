## Text

Team announcement: On Thursday at 10:00 a.m., we will <em>demo</em> the new customer dashboard for the sales and support teams. Please review the <em>deck</em> in advance, especially the <em>slide</em> that explains the main user problem.

During the session, Maya will <em>walk through</em> the product <em>roadmap</em> and <em>highlight</em> the features planned for the next release. The discussion will be <em>data-driven</em>, so bring any <em>insight</em> from recent customer calls or usage reports.

We will also decide which requests are <em>feasible</em> before launch and which should move to a later phase. The final <em>slide</em> will include a clear <em>call to action</em> for stakeholders and one key <em>takeaway</em> for each department.

After the meeting, the product team will <em>follow up</em> with notes, open questions, and next steps. Please arrive on time and be ready to share concise <em>feedback</em>.
