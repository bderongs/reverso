## Text

After a long train ride, Maya dragged her <em>suitcase</em> through the glass doors and into the bright <em>lobby</em>. She was relieved to see the <em>front desk</em> straight ahead, with a smiling <em>receptionist</em> helping another guest.

When it was her turn to <em>check in</em>, Maya gave her passport and confirmed her <em>booking</em>. The <em>receptionist</em> handed her a key card, wrote down her <em>room number</em>, and explained that breakfast was served on the second floor.

A <em>porter</em> offered to carry her <em>luggage</em>, but a cheerful <em>bellhop</em> had already placed her bags on a small trolley. On the way to the elevator, he pointed out the restaurant, the <em>spa</em>, and the emergency exits.

In her room, Maya tipped the <em>bellhop</em>, locked the door, and opened the curtains to see the city below. She decided to <em>unpack</em> only the essentials, take a quick shower, and finally <em>settle</em> in before meeting her colleagues downstairs.
