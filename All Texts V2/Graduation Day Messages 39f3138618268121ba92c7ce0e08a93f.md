## Text

The Office of Student Affairs is pleased to announce that this year’s <em>graduation</em> ceremony will take place on Saturday, June 15, at 10:00 a.m. in the main <em>auditorium</em>. All undergraduate candidates who have met the requirements for <em>completion</em> of their <em>degree</em> should arrive by 8:30 a.m. for <em>registration</em> and seating.

The <em>dean</em> will welcome students, families, and guests, followed by remarks from selected <em>faculty</em> members and our <em>valedictorian</em>, Maya Chen. Each graduate will cross the stage to receive a <em>diploma</em>, marking an important academic <em>accomplishment</em> and the beginning of a new professional chapter.

Due to limited seating, <em>attendance</em> is restricted to ticketed guests only, and doors will close promptly at 9:50 a.m. We are also honored to welcome Daniel Ruiz, a distinguished <em>alumnus</em>, who will deliver the keynote address and share reflections on university life after commencement.
