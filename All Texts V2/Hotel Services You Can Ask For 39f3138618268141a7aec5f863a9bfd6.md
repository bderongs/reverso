## Text

When staying at a hotel, many guests use only the basics, but most properties offer extra services that can make a trip smoother. Before you arrive, check which <em>amenities</em> are included, such as a <em>gym</em>, <em>spa</em> access, free bottled water, or a business center.

If you are tired after a long journey, <em>room service</em> can bring dinner directly to your door. You can also request a <em>wake-up call</em> from reception, especially if you have an early flight or an important meeting.

<em>Housekeeping</em> usually cleans the room daily, replaces <em>towels</em>, and restocks toiletries. If something is missing, ask the <em>maid</em> politely, or contact the <em>front desk</em> for help.

For clothing care, hotels may offer <em>laundry</em> and <em>dry-cleaning</em>, often with same-day options. Some luxury hotels also provide <em>shoeshine</em> service and a <em>valet</em> who can park your car or assist with <em>luggage</em>.

The <em>concierge</em> is useful for restaurant <em>bookings</em>, theater tickets, or local travel advice. In high-end suites, a <em>butler</em> may <em>unpack</em> bags, serve drinks, or arrange personal requests.
