Subject: Transportation Options During Your <em>Stay</em>

Dear Ms. Carter,

Thank you for confirming your <em>reservation</em> with us. Our <em>address</em> is 48 King Street, in a quiet <em>neighborhood</em> close to the riverfront and several cafés.

For local travel, we offer a complimentary <em>shuttle</em> to the city center every hour from 8 a.m. to 10 p.m. The <em>concierge</em> can also arrange a taxi or recommend walking routes to attractions in the <em>vicinity</em>.

If you plan to explore the <em>outskirts</em>, a <em>car rental</em> <em>desk</em> is available in the <em>lobby</em>. Please note that each <em>rental</em> includes basic insurance, and advance <em>booking</em> is recommended during weekends.

We also provide secure <em>parking</em> behind the hotel for guests who arrive by car. If you prefer extra convenience, our <em>valet</em> service can collect your vehicle at the main entrance.

Please let us know if you need further assistance before your arrival.

Kind regards,
Maria Jensen
Guest Services Manager
