## Text

Choosing the right <em>outfit</em> for an interview can help you feel confident before you even answer the first question. In most offices, a <em>formal</em> look is still a safe choice, especially for corporate roles.

For men, a clean <em>shirt</em> with a <em>tailored</em> <em>suit</em>, a simple <em>tie</em>, and dark <em>shoes</em> creates a <em>polished</em> impression. A matching <em>belt</em> is a small detail, but it helps the whole <em>outfit</em> look more professional.

For women, a <em>blouse</em> with a <em>blazer</em> and trousers or a knee-length <em>skirt</em> is a <em>classic</em> option. Neutral colours such as navy, grey, black, or cream are easy to style and rarely distract from what you are saying.

If the company describes its <em>dress</em> code as <em>smart casual</em>, you can relax the look slightly without appearing careless. For example, a neat <em>blazer</em> over a <em>plain</em> <em>shirt</em> or <em>blouse</em> can balance comfort with professionalism.

Before leaving home, check that everything is clean, ironed, and comfortable. The best interview <em>outfit</em> should support your confidence, not make you worry about your clothes.
