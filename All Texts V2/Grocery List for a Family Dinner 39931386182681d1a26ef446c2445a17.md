Subject: What to buy before Saturday

Hi Marta,

I checked the kitchen this morning, and we’ve nearly <em>run out</em> of olive oil, so please get a small bottle if you pass the market. We also need a <em>tin</em> of tomatoes, a bag of <em>frozen</em> peas, and two <em>ripe</em> avocados for the salad.

For the starter, buy some <em>smoked</em> salmon, <em>pickled</em> cucumbers, and a <em>fresh</em> loaf of <em>bread</em>. I’ll add a <em>handful</em> of herbs, a <em>pinch</em> of sea salt, and a <em>dash</em> of lemon juice to make it brighter.

For dessert, could you pick up vanilla ice cream? One <em>scoop</em> per person should be enough after such a big meal. Please also avoid buying <em>raw</em> prawns this time, as Dad prefers them <em>cooked</em>.

If the berry sauce turns out well, we can <em>bottle</em> the extra and keep it for next week.

Thanks,
Elena
