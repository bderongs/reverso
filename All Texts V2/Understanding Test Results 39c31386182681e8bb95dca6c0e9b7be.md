Subject: <em>Follow-up</em> on Your Recent Tests

Dear Mr. Lewis,

I am writing to explain the results from your <em>clinical</em> <em>assessment</em> last Friday. Your blood <em>sample</em> showed <em>mild</em> <em>inflammation</em>, but the throat <em>specimen</em> we took after we <em>swabbed</em> the area did not <em>detect</em> any bacterial <em>infection</em>.

The <em>ultrasound</em> <em>scan</em> of your abdomen was clear, which is reassuring. However, the <em>MRI</em> showed a small area that needs closer observation before we can make a final <em>diagnosis</em>.

At this stage, there is no sign of anything <em>urgent</em>, but early <em>detection</em> is important. To be thorough, we may need to <em>biopsy</em> the area if it changes or causes <em>symptoms</em>.

Please book a follow-up <em>appointment</em> in four weeks so we can review your progress and decide whether further tests are needed. If you develop <em>fever</em>, <em>severe</em> pain, or swelling, contact the <em>clinic</em> immediately.

Kind regards,
Dr. Anita Shah
