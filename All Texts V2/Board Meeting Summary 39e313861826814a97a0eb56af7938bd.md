## Text

At Tuesday’s quarterly meeting, directors addressed the <em>elephant in the room</em>: slowing sales in the European market. The chair asked the team to focus on the <em>big picture</em> rather than short-term blame.

Several managers argued that the company is <em>at a crossroads</em>, with its expansion strategy now <em>up in the air</em>. To <em>play devil’s advocate</em>, the finance director warned that entering new markets too quickly could weaken cash flow.

After two hours of debate, the issue seemed to <em>boil down to</em> pricing, customer trust, and supply delays. The <em>bottom line</em> is that the company must protect profit margins while rebuilding confidence among key clients.

A proposal to redesign the website was kept <em>on the back burner</em>, while the marketing campaign was formally <em>put on the back burner</em> until next quarter. For now, senior leadership will <em>call the shots</em> on budget cuts, but <em>the ball is in the sales team’s court</em> to deliver a revised growth plan by Friday.
