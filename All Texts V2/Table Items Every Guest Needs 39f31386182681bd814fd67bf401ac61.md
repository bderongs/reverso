## Text

Announcement: To improve service speed and keep each setting consistent, our dining room team will prepare every <em>table</em> before guests are seated. Please check that all <em>tableware</em> is clean, polished, and placed according to the floor plan.

Each setting should include <em>dinnerware</em> for the reserved <em>menu</em>: one main <em>dish</em> <em>plate</em>, a side <em>plate</em> with a <em>saucer</em> when coffee or tea is <em>ordered</em>, and the correct <em>silverware</em>. If guests request steak or seafood, replace the standard <em>cutlery</em> with the appropriate knife or fork.

For <em>drinks</em>, place one water <em>glass</em> or <em>tumbler</em> at the top right of the setting, and add a <em>wineglass</em> only when wine service is expected. Every cloth <em>napkin</em> should be folded neatly, secured with a <em>napkin ring</em>, and positioned beside the <em>plate</em>.

Shared items must be centered and easy to reach: one <em>salt shaker</em>, one <em>pepper shaker</em>, and any requested <em>condiment</em>, including <em>hot sauce</em>. Please use a <em>coaster</em> under every bottled <em>beverage</em> to protect the <em>tables</em>.
