Subject: Advice on My <em>Application</em> for the Marketing Assistant <em>Vacancy</em>

Dear Ms. Carter,

I hope you are well. I am planning to <em>apply</em> for the Marketing Assistant <em>vacancy</em> at Greenline Media, and I would appreciate your advice on my <em>cover letter</em> before I send my <em>application</em>.

I want the letter to highlight my <em>work experience</em> without simply repeating my CV. During my <em>internship</em>, my main <em>achievement</em> was increasing newsletter engagement by 18%, and I would like to present this <em>accomplishment</em> in a way that helps me <em>stand out</em>.

The role seems highly <em>desirable</em> because it combines <em>creative</em> writing, campaign planning, and client communication. I believe it would be a <em>worthwhile</em> opportunity for me to <em>develop</em> professionally while contributing real <em>enthusiasm</em> to the team.

If you feel comfortable doing so, could you also <em>vouch</em> for my reliability and attention to detail? Your feedback would mean a great deal to me.

Best regards,

Daniel
