Subject: Quick ideas for Saturday

Hi Maya,

If the weather holds, let’s <em>hit the road</em> around 8 a.m. and get to the lake <em>in no time</em>. I know we only do this <em>once in a blue moon</em>, so I think it’s <em>worth a shot</em> to book a small cabin instead of rushing back the same day.

I’ve checked the forecast <em>on and off</em> all morning, but it keeps changing, so we may need to <em>play it by ear</em>. If it pours, we can take a <em>rain check</em> and try again next weekend.

You’re welcome to arrive at my place Friday night; just <em>make yourself at home</em> and <em>help yourself</em> to anything in the fridge. I’ll pack sandwiches, but <em>sooner or later</em> we’ll probably stop for coffee anyway.

<em>Never mind</em> bringing extra blankets—I found two in the closet. Let’s <em>take it easy</em>, keep the plan simple, and enjoy a quiet break.

See you soon,
Daniel
