Subject: Follow-Up on Your Offer

Dear Ms. Patel,

Thank you for the <em>job offer</em> and for the confidence you have shown in me. I am excited about the opportunity to <em>employ</em> my project management <em>skills</em> at GreenLine Solutions and help <em>fulfill</em> the team’s goals.

Before I formally accept, I would like to <em>negotiate</em> the <em>salary</em> package. Based on my experience, the responsibilities of the role, and the market rate for a sought-after <em>candidate</em> in this field, I believe a slightly higher figure would be more <em>suitable</em>.

My <em>objective</em> is to reach an agreement that feels fair to both sides. The main <em>takeaway</em> from our conversations is that we share the same expectations for quality, growth, and long-term commitment.

My <em>bottom line</em> is not only compensation, but also the chance to <em>pursue</em> meaningful work with a strong team. I would prefer not to <em>turn down</em> this opportunity, and I hope we can discuss the details further.

Kind regards,

Daniel Morris
