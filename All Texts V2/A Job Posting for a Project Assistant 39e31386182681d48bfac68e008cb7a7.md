## Text

Greenbridge Learning Center is pleased to announce a new <em>vacancy</em> for a Project Assistant to support our community education programs. This opportunity is ideal for <em>candidates</em> who are currently <em>job hunting</em> and want to gain practical experience in a busy nonprofit office.

According to the <em>job description</em>, the successful <em>applicant</em> will <em>coordinate</em> meetings, <em>administer</em> participant records, <em>facilitate</em> communication between trainers and volunteers, and <em>update</em> project calendars each week. The assistant will also be <em>responsible</em> for preparing simple reports and keeping shared documents accurate and accessible.

We are looking for an <em>organized</em>, <em>punctual</em>, and <em>dependable</em> person who can work well with a small team and respond professionally to changing priorities. The ideal <em>candidate</em> should be <em>efficient</em>, comfortable using email and spreadsheets, and able to <em>manage</em> several tasks without losing attention to detail.

This is a full-time position from Monday to Friday, with occasional evening events. The <em>salary</em> is <em>competitive</em> for an entry-level role, and training will be provided; interested <em>applicants</em> should send a CV and <em>cover letter</em> by Friday, 18 October.
