## Text

Dear guests, welcome to our <em>resort</em>. We hope you enjoy a relaxing stay and take full advantage of our beautiful <em>beach</em>, which is open daily from 6:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m.

For your comfort, each room includes a fresh <em>towel</em> for <em>beach</em> use, and extra <em>towels</em> are available at the pool desk. You may reserve a <em>beach chair</em> and <em>beach umbrella</em> through reception, especially on weekends when the <em>shore</em> can become <em>crowded</em>.

Please remember to apply <em>sunscreen</em> regularly and follow the safety instructions posted near the water. A <em>lifeguard</em> is on duty from 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., and <em>swimming</em> beyond the marked area is not permitted.

Guests may book a <em>kayak</em>, join a <em>sailboat</em> tour, or take a quiet walk along the <em>pier</em>. In the evening, we recommend finding a <em>peaceful</em> spot to watch the <em>sunset</em> over the water.
