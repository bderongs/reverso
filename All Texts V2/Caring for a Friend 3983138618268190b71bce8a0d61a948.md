Subject: I’m here for you

Dear Maya,

I heard the news about your father, and I’m writing <em>with a heavy heart</em>. Please accept my deepest <em>sympathy</em>; I can only imagine the <em>sorrow</em> and <em>grief</em> your family is feeling right now.

When my aunt died, I remember how <em>bereaved</em> and lost we all felt, and some days my cousin was almost <em>inconsolable</em>. People often offered <em>pity</em>, but what helped most was quiet company, warm meals, and the chance to <em>mourn</em> without being rushed.

If you want to talk, <em>shed tears</em>, or sit in silence, I’ll come over anytime. I was <em>touched</em> by how lovingly you cared for him, and I hope you feel <em>comforted</em> knowing he was surrounded by devotion.

There may be little <em>solace</em> in words today, but you do not have to carry this alone. I <em>have a heavy heart</em> for you, and I’ll check in tomorrow.

With love,
Emma
