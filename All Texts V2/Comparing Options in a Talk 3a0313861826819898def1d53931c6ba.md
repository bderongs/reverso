## Text

Announcement: Next Thursday at 3 p.m., the Learning Centre will host a short workshop on presenting choices clearly to an audience. We will practise phrases such as “<em>on the one hand</em>” and “<em>on the other hand</em>” to introduce two possible solutions, <em>whereas</em> “<em>however</em>” and “<em>nevertheless</em>” will help you show a change in direction.

Participants will compare <em>slide</em> designs, speaking styles, and ways to answer questions. <em>In contrast</em>, we will also examine weak examples; <em>by contrast</em>, strong examples will show how confident speakers guide listeners <em>instead</em> of confusing them.

The session is open to all intermediate and advanced English learners. <em>Similarly</em>, staff members who give updates in meetings are welcome, and <em>likewise</em>, students preparing project presentations may attend; <em>even so</em>, places are limited, so please register at reception by Tuesday.
