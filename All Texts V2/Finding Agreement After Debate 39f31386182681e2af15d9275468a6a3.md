Subject: <em>Next Steps</em> After Today’s Planning Meeting

Hi team,

Thank you for the honest discussion this morning. Although we started with different <em>priorities</em>, I think we managed to <em>find common ground</em> on the project <em>timeline</em> and the budget limits.

There is now a clear <em>consensus</em> that we should launch in phases rather than all at once. This approach gives us a practical <em>middle ground</em> and should help us gain <em>buy-in</em> from both the sales and support teams.

To move forward, we still need to <em>negotiate</em> the final delivery dates with the design <em>group</em>. I believe we can <em>compromise</em> on the testing period if they <em>agree</em> to <em>meet halfway</em> on the review schedule.

Please send me your <em>agreement</em> or <em>concerns</em> by Friday afternoon. Once we have <em>approval</em> from department leads, I will request formal <em>sign-off</em> from senior management.

Your <em>commitment</em> to this plan is important, so please make sure your teams are fully <em>on board</em> before our next meeting.

Best,
Laura
