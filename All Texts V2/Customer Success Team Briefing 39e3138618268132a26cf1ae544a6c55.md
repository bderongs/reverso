## Text

Announcement: Starting Monday, we will launch a revised onboarding process for all new enterprise clients. Please review the updated checklist so everyone stays <em>in the loop</em> and can help new teammates get <em>up to speed</em> quickly.

Several new specialists are coming <em>on board</em> this quarter, and they will need time to <em>learn the ropes</em>. During this period, team leads may ask you to <em>wear many hats</em>, from answering urgent tickets to joining client kickoff calls.

Each team member is expected to <em>pull your weight</em> and <em>bring to the table</em> practical ideas that improve the customer experience. We also need to <em>cover all bases</em> by confirming timelines, risks, and responsibilities before every major account launch.

Our goal is simple: <em>deliver the goods</em> consistently and <em>go the extra mile</em> when clients face pressure. If we work together and share feedback openly, we can <em>raise the bar</em> for service across the department.
