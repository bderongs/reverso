## Text

Dear parents and guardians,

We have been informed that several students have visited the nurse this week with a <em>mild</em> skin <em>rash</em>. In most cases, the <em>rash</em> appears <em>harmless</em>, but some children have reported an <em>itch</em>, a small <em>blister</em>, or a <em>lump</em> that looks red or <em>puffy</em>.

At this time, there is no confirmed <em>infection</em>, and the <em>condition</em> does not appear to be highly <em>contagious</em>. However, as a <em>precaution</em>, we ask families to check their children’s skin before school and contact a doctor if they notice unusual <em>inflammation</em>, spreading spots, or discomfort.

To help <em>prevent</em> further <em>irritation</em>, students should <em>avoid</em> scratching affected areas and should not share towels, sports clothing, or personal items. Please remind your child to wash their hands regularly and tell a teacher or the nurse if any skin changes appear during the day.

Thank you for helping us keep our school community healthy and safe.
