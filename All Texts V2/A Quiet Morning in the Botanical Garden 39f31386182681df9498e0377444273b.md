## Text

Last Saturday, I arrived at the <em>botanical garden</em> just after it opened, when the paths were still empty and the air felt cool. The <em>surroundings</em> were <em>peaceful</em>, and the soft light made the whole <em>landscape</em> look almost magical.

I first walked through the <em>arboretum</em>, where tall maples, oaks, and cedars stood like quiet guardians. A <em>picturesque</em> wooden <em>bridge</em> over a small pond immediately <em>caught my eye</em>, especially with <em>colorful</em> flowers reflected in the water.

One <em>beautiful</em> <em>feature</em> of the garden was a narrow trail lined with herbs and fragrant roses. I stopped several times, letting my <em>gaze</em> rest on butterflies moving slowly from one blossom to another.

By the time I reached the glasshouse, the morning had become warm but still <em>serene</em>. I sat on a bench, listened to the birds, and felt grateful for this calm place in the middle of the city.
