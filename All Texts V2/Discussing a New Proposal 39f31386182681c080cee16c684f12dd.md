## Text

During Tuesday’s management meeting, the team reviewed a <em>proposal</em> to introduce flexible working hours across the company. The operations manager said early <em>feedback</em> from staff was positive, but she <em>advised</em> leaders to consider possible effects on customer service.

Several department heads <em>supported</em> the idea, <em>arguing</em> that flexibility could improve motivation and reduce staff turnover. However, the finance director <em>challenged</em> the <em>claim</em> that the change would save money, and he <em>pointed out</em> that extra scheduling software might be required.

The HR manager <em>recommended</em> a three-month trial before making a final <em>decision</em>. She also <em>suggested</em> an <em>alternative</em>: allowing each department to choose its own core hours while keeping shared meeting times.

By the end of the discussion, most participants were ready to <em>endorse</em> a limited pilot program. The board will review the results in September and decide whether the policy should become permanent.
