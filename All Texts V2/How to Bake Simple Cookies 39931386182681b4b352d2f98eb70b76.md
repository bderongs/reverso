## Text

A reliable <em>recipe</em> can turn a quiet afternoon into a warm, fragrant kitchen experience. Before you begin, <em>preheat</em> the <em>oven</em> to 180°C and line a <em>tray</em> with <em>baking paper</em> so the <em>cookies</em> will not stick.

In a large <em>bowl</em>, <em>measure</em> 100 grams of butter and 150 grams of sugar, then <em>mix</em> them until smooth. Add one egg, a <em>teaspoon</em> of vanilla, and a <em>tablespoon</em> of milk, <em>stirring</em> gently before adding flour to form a <em>soft</em> <em>dough</em>.

<em>Roll</em> small pieces of <em>dough</em> between your hands and place each <em>cookie</em> on the prepared <em>tray</em>, leaving space between them. <em>Bake</em> for 10 to 12 minutes, or until the edges look lightly golden.

When the <em>tray</em> comes out of the <em>oven</em>, let the <em>cookies</em> <em>cool down</em> for a few minutes before moving them to a <em>plate</em>. Enjoy them with tea, coffee, or a <em>glass</em> of milk while they are still slightly warm.
